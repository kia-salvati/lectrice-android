# S5: Galaxy S26 Ultra over wireless adb (2026-10-25)

Nothing committed. Phone: SM-S948B, Snapdragon SM8850 (`canoe`, 8 cores), 11 GB RAM, Android 17 / API 37, 4 KB pages.
Build `sherpa1` only (sherpa-onnx 1.13.8; sherpa 2.0 was rejected in S6). The APK was rebuilt with the models inside
(`-Plectrice.bundleModels=true`, 539.2 MB), because `lectrice-ffi` had changed since the S7 build. The core comes from
the local `lectrice` checkout (0.3.0 on `feature/desktop-support`, through the path override), so the result files say
`coreRevision v0.2.0-dirty`.

**Every number here is unplugged** (the phone was never on a charger). The battery went from 53 % to 24 % over the
whole session, and its temperature from 26 °C to 45 °C.

Results (ids, counts and durations only; checked: no book text, no file names):
- `docs/results/S26U-sherpa1-run1.json`: install, model copy, engine, import, and the bench of fp32/int8 × 2/4/6/8 threads.
- `docs/results/S26U-sherpa1-run2.json`: the 10-minute soak (fp32, 8 threads), Play 2 min on both PDFs, and the
  accelerators.
- The accelerator WAVs (gitignored) are in `test-data/ear-test/S26U/{fp32,int8}-{cpu,xnnpack,nnapi}.wav`. All six came
  from the CPU.

The PDFs went to the phone as `large.pdf` (51.6 MB, 368 pages) and `medium.pdf` (8.4 MB, 429 pages), the same two books
as on the S10+.

## How the run went

- `spike/run-phone.sh 192.168.70.154:46001 <large> <medium> 2,4,6,8` ran these steps cleanly:
  1. install over Wi-Fi;
  2. first launch: the model copy took 0.3 s for fp32 and 0.13 s for int8;
  3. engine;
  4. import;
  5. the whole bench.
- **Wireless adb dropped** at about 14:18, right when the bench finished. The phone switched Wireless debugging off.
  While it was off, the user had to open Settings to turn it back on, and **the spike app's process died** in the
  background.
- The rest ran by hand with the same `am start … --es step …` commands, on a new port (`:45933`), in a new app session:
  1. the soak first (on the coordinator's priority list it came before the accelerators, and the battery was already
     low);
  2. Play on both PDFs;
  3. the accelerators last.
- The script's "fastest combination" step (the result-file fix from S7) **was not exercised** this time, because adb
  dropped before it. Its logic, the newest session file by name, looks right. The app's own pick was the same:
  "best threads: {FP32=8, INT8=2}".
- The app stayed in the foreground for every measured step: I checked `topResumedActivity` every 2–3 min, and the screen
  never locked.
- The phone has a screen lock, so the **cold engine load was skipped** (as on the S10+).
- Small spike bug: `providerLines` sometimes catches the app's own previous `LectriceSpike` line along with sherpa's
  line. The `providerEngaged` flags are still right.

### Why the large-PDF Play buffered when the RTF is 0.6

- The large book has many very short sentences: lines of under 1 s each.
- Play ran right after the soak, with the phone at SEVERE (about 43 °C).
- Each sentence open took 2–9 s, even for 0.6–1 s of audio. That is much worse than the hot bench (RTF 0.66 on whole
  sentences).
- `readyAheadMs` was 0 for every open, so the read-ahead never got ahead.
- Same finding as on the S10+: ExoPlayer's own preloading and the core's read-ahead fight over the one engine, and each
  short call carries a fixed cost.
- The medium book has longer sentences. Under the same heat it played with no gaps, and the read-ahead pulled ahead.
- **Fix for M3:** one generation queue, where the player waits on the read-ahead's result instead of starting a second
  call. Short sentences should also be merged into batches before they go to the engine. This is a product-code issue,
  not a speed limit.

### Threads

- Every bench row ran in sequence while the phone warmed up, so heat skews the thread ranking: 4 and 6 threads ran in
  the middle of the heat ramp. Cool, 2 threads (0.478) and 8 threads (0.465) are about equal.
- 8 threads is the fastest, but it keeps all cores busy and reached SEVERE in 85 s. 2 threads gives almost the same
  speed on a quarter of the cores.
- **Recommendation:** default fp32 at **4 threads** on S26-class phones.
- **Follow-up:** before M3 fixes the default, run a 2-thread and a 4-thread soak (cool phone, unplugged) and compare
  sustained RTF and mAh per audio-minute. Not done today because the battery got too low.
- At 1× listening, the engine only needs to run about 60 % of the time. Pausing generation once it is 10 min ahead (the
  desktop's read-ahead rule) also limits the heat.
