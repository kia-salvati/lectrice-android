## Router off (the user will test)

adb runs over the same Wi-Fi, so this needs a person. About 10 minutes:

1. Laptop: start Lectrice, Settings → Phone → "Let my phone use this computer" on (the S10+ is paired with it, or pair it
   now). Phone: play a long book until the player sheet says "Making audio on *laptop* · 10 min ready" or more.
2. Note the time and **switch the router off** (power). Keep listening.
3. Expect: **no gap** in the voice; within ~15 s the sheet line says "Computer not reachable · N min ready" and one
   snackbar appears; playback goes on (the phone makes the audio itself, starting at the far end of the buffer).
4. Leave it 2–3 minutes, then **switch the router on**. When both have Wi-Fi again, within ~10 s of the phone's Wi-Fi
   being back the line says "Making audio on *laptop*" again.
5. Report: any gap or stop, roughly how long until "not reachable", and how long until "Making audio" came back.
   Optional afterwards: `adb logcat -d -s Lectrice > router-off.txt` (only ids, counts and states; no book text).
